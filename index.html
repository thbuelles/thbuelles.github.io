About

I'm a research scientist in the San Francisco Bay Area. I currently work on foundation models and computational methods for de novo protein design at Pythia Labs.

My background is in pure mathematics. Before dabbling with proteins, I was a Taussky–Todd fellow at Caltech working on algebraic geometry and string theory. I did my PhD at ETH Zürich.

Research

See Journal of Algebraic Geometry, Forum of Mathematics, and Manuscripta Mathematica for some of my papers, mostly on K3 surfaces, derived categories, and enumerative geometry.

Teaching

I was an instructor for mathematics at Caltech, ETH Zürich, and the University of Bonn from 2016 to 2023. I taught courses in Linear Algebra, Commutative Algebra, Group Theory, Representation Theory, Galois Theory, Algebraic Curves, and Algebraic Geometry. See the complete list of courses here.
